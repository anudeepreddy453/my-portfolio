Privacy & retention

Precise GPS is stored only after the visitor uses the browser’s own permission prompt. Click a coordinate to open that point in Google Maps. IP location is an estimate and is labeled that way.

Coordinates are not a home address and are not an identity. Two visits from one network are listed as separate sessions.

Use a visitor record to delete that record, delete GPS coordinates, or revoke the session. Visitors can also email the address published on the privacy policy.
